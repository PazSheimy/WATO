Research

SSRN working paper · 2026

Out-of-Sample Evaluation of an Intraday Momentum Strategy for the S&P 500 ETF (SPY)

An independent replication of a published intraday momentum strategy on 1-minute SPY data. The in-sample result reproduces almost exactly (Sharpe 1.34 over 2015 to 2024), but on the two years that postdate the original study the edge decays: Sharpe 0.39, a 9.4% return against 29.5% for buy-and-hold, and a statistically significant drop in risk-adjusted returns.

Paz, S. (2026). Out-of-Sample Evaluation of an Intraday Momentum Strategy for the S&P 500 ETF (SPY). SSRN Working Paper. doi:10.2139/ssrn.7290621
