Where we work

Industries

We take on problems where the right model is the difference between a good quarter and a bad one.

Construction

Cut overruns and delays before the first pour. We build models that forecast cost, schedule, and material risk.

  • Cost forecasting — predict budgets from historical project data and flag overruns early.
  • Resource planning — optimize crew, equipment, and sequencing.
  • Demand forecasting — anticipate material needs and prevent shortages.
  • Waste reduction — identify where excess and rework are eating margin.

Hospitals & Healthcare

Better care, lower waste. We help hospitals forecast demand, catch fraud, and deploy capacity where it matters.

  • Patient-flow forecasting — staffing and capacity plans driven by admission predictions.
  • Fraud & claims review — anomaly detection for billing and medical coding.
  • Budget optimization — inventory and spend models that cut waste without cutting care.
  • ICU bed management — forecast demand and avoid costly over- or under-capacity.
  • ER wait-time reduction — predict peak hours and match staffing to real demand.

Real Estate

AI and blockchain tooling for valuation, transactions, and underwriting.

  • Market trend modeling — forecast prices and surface investment signals.
  • Automated valuation — price properties from location, demand, and market data.
  • Smart contracts — transparent, auditable property transactions on-chain.
  • Mortgage risk scoring — sharper lending decisions, faster approvals.
  • Tenant screening — surface risk without relying on noisy proxies.

Logistics & Supply Chain

Cut delivery cost, reduce stockouts, and keep fleets moving.

  • Demand forecasting — balance stock against real demand signals.
  • Fleet & route optimization — lower fuel burn and on-time misses.
  • Live shipment tracking — ML-backed ETAs and exception alerts.
  • Predictive maintenance — catch failures before they ground a truck.
  • Autonomous operations — from smart sortation to last-mile automation.

Blockchain

We integrate blockchain where it actually earns its keep — transparency, provenance, and settlement.

  • Secure transactions — decentralized ledgers for auditable payments.
  • Smart contracts — self-executing agreements that remove reconciliation work.
  • Supply-chain tracking — provenance for food safety and pharmaceuticals.
  • Identity verification — stronger KYC without hoarding personal data.
  • DeFi integrations — selectively, when they solve a real cost or access problem.

Finance & Trading

Statistical models for traders, funds, and treasuries that need to measure risk, not guess it.

  • Market prediction — trend, sentiment, and macro signals combined in one model.
  • Algorithmic trading — ML- and probability-driven strategies from research to live.
  • Risk management — VaR, stress tests, and drawdown controls built to hold up.
  • Anomaly detection — spot unusual market or account behavior before it costs you.
  • Portfolio optimization — Monte Carlo and Bayesian allocation, not naive mean-variance.

Restaurants & Food Service

Reduce waste, tighten margins, and price smarter with models built for real operators.

  • Demand forecasting — order what you'll sell, not what you hope to.
  • Dynamic pricing — menu pricing that responds to cost and demand.
  • Table turnover — seating strategies that lift revenue at peak.
  • Supply-chain planning — avoid 86ing items and prevent spoilage.
  • Loss prevention — detect unusual transactions and shrink patterns.
